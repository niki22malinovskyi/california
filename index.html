H1 A LITTLE ADVENTURE, CLOSER THAT YOU THINK

You don’t have to hop on a plane or empty your wallet to unwind.

Just a short drive from Sacramento or the Bay Area, the Yuba Foothills are packed with rolling hills, quirky charm, and local flavor that’s anything but ordinary.

One moment you’re sipping wine at a hilltop vineyard, the next you’re blending essential oils, walking with llamas, doodling in a sunny garden, listening to live music, or lingering over coffee and warm pastries at a cozy café.

It’s easy, it’s beautiful — and it’s all waiting just off the beaten path.
